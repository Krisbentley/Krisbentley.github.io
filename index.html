What I do

When the usual answers aren't enough.

From operational uncertainty to emerging technology, I work on clearly bounded problems where the cost of a weak assumption can be significant.

01 / CAPABILITY

Investigate the real problem

Clarify the claim, examine the evidence, identify contradictions and isolate what is known from what is assumed.

02 / CAPABILITY

Design a practical response

Turn findings into an actionable remediation plan with responsibilities, dependencies and decision points.

03 / CAPABILITY

Verify the outcome

Retest against agreed criteria and document what is supported, unresolved or outside scope.

Operating principle

Evidence before gate.

No premature conclusions. No borrowed authority. No confusing a promising prototype with an independently proven system. Decisions move forward when the evidence supports them.
