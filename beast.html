BEAST AI: challenge claims, retain the record.

A working research capability for structured investigation, adversarial challenge and traceable technical reasoning—used selectively to support Kris Bentley’s independent consultancy, never to replace professional judgement.

Consultancy framing

Useful where the record is complex and the answer must be defensible.

BEAST AI helps organise authorised evidence, test competing explanations, preserve adverse findings and show how a conclusion was reached. It is suited to bounded investigations, assurance reviews, technical remediation and special projects where an unexamined assumption has a real cost.

The consultancy remains founder-led. Kris defines the scope, selects the methods, directs the work and remains responsible for the professional judgement and deliverables communicated to a client.

Clear boundary: BEAST AI is proprietary research and supporting technical capability. It is not presented as a certified product, a regulated decision-maker or a substitute for appropriately authorised professional advice.
Latest developments

From a frozen baseline to a working bounded loop.

IMPLEMENTED

Multi-agent challenge architecture

Worker, manager, executive and challenger roles separate task execution, coordination, decision review and adversarial scrutiny. The purpose is disciplined challenge, not autonomous authority.

IMPLEMENTED

Corpus Callosum & evidence ledger

Bidirectional retrieval connects a question to relevant records and records back to the reasoning trail. A hash-linked evidence ledger supports provenance, checkpoints and later review.

IMPLEMENTED

Bounded engineering loop

A local engineering / test / repair loop can make and assess constrained changes, retain failure evidence and escalate beyond its authority rather than silently promoting an unverified result.

Experiments, honestly reported

Promising narrow signal; no generalisation claim.

In two trials, a narrow memory measure improved by 16.67 percentage points. That is an observed experiment result, not a claim of broad capability, reliability or deployment readiness.

EXPERIMENT

Narrow memory

+16.67 percentage points across two trials. The measure is specific and should be read within its test conditions.

INCONCLUSIVE

Broader generalisation

Current evidence does not establish a generalisation improvement beyond the narrow experimental setting.

INCONCLUSIVE / FAILED

Roadmap

Build evidence before expanding authority.

Next work focuses on independent review, leakage-controlled evaluation, reciprocal-case checks and further validation of bounded engineering controls. Any move toward broader use remains conditional on evidence, review and explicit human approval.

Implementation, experiment and roadmap labels on this page are deliberate: they distinguish working components from results still being tested and work not yet claimed.
