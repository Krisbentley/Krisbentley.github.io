What it is—and how it works

Kris Bentley provides independent technical consultancy, complex problem-solving and specialist project support. BEAST AI is his proprietary evidence-led methodology and supporting technical capability, developed to strengthen investigation, analysis, challenge and traceability.

The consultancy combines practical experience, structured investigation, appropriate technology and direct professional judgement to deliver clearly defined outcomes. Supporting systems are selected and applied according to each engagement’s requirements, with human oversight, evidence integrity and transparent reporting throughout.

Professional responsibility: Findings and recommendations remain bounded by the agreed scope, available evidence and applicable professional or regulatory requirements. Experimental capabilities are not presented as independently certified or production-proven.

Why it matters

The guiding principle is simple: retain adverse evidence, identify uncertainty and never advance a claim further than the record justifies.
