Method

A method built for uncertainty.

Define the question. Gather the record. Challenge the explanation. Test the answer.

01 — Define the decision

Establish the problem, desired outcome, boundaries, stakeholders, constraints and criteria for success.

02 — Establish the evidence

Collect authorised material, record provenance, identify gaps and distinguish observations from assertions.

03 — Challenge assumptions

Seek contradictions and alternative explanations. Preserve negative findings rather than smoothing them away.

04 — Develop remediation

Prioritise viable corrective actions and specify how each will be evaluated.

05 — Verify and report

Retest where feasible, provide traceable findings and clearly state residual risk and limitations.
