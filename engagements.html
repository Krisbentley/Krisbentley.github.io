Commercial engagement

Clear scope. Paid work. Defined outcomes.

A flexible gate-based approach, with the deliverables and commercial terms agreed before work begins.

GATE 01

Initial assessment

Problem framing, initial evidence review, practical scoping and an agreed next-step recommendation. Site attendance where appropriate.

GATE 02

Structured investigation

Deeper evidence examination, competing-hypothesis testing, findings and prioritised options.

GATE 03

Remediation & retesting

Design and analytical implementation of corrective work where feasible, followed by verification against agreed criteria.

GATE 04

Extended or continuing support

Defined follow-through, independent challenge, further verification or a separately agreed retained scope.

Commercial note: Fees, scope, site visits, mobilisation, expenses and acceptance criteria are confirmed in a written proposal. No fixed price on this page constitutes an offer or quote.

The work may be structured as a fixed-fee project, time-bounded consultancy or ongoing defined engagement, depending on the problem.
