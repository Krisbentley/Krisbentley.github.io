International delivery

Go to the evidence. Keep ownership of the outcome.

A practical blend of site attendance and remote delivery, adapted to the problem rather than the other way around.

On-site when it adds value

Where authorised and feasible, a short site visit can establish context, observe operations, meet responsible teams and gather permitted evidence.

Substantive work from the UK

Investigation, analytical remediation, retesting, verification and reporting can continue remotely where the work and client systems permit. Physical interventions requiring local presence can be coordinated with suitably qualified local personnel without automatically transferring responsibility for the contracted analytical work.

Clear legal and practical boundaries

Travel, access permissions, visa and work authorisation, confidentiality, safety, data handling and insurance are assessed before any overseas engagement is accepted.

Availability for international attendance is conditional, not guaranteed. Remote-only engagements are also possible.
